TeachyJobs — Complete Guide

🔍 Browsing & Searching

  • Ctrl+K — Focus search bar instantly from anywhere
  • Type any keyword: school name, subject, area, or contact
  • Results update live as you type
  • Click any job card to see full details including the raw Arabic ad

🎛️ Filters

  • Area — Filter by one of 56 Egyptian areas
  • Subject — Filter by teaching subject (51 types)
  • Year — Browse data from 2020 to 2024
  • Duplicates — Show all, unique only, or duplicates only
  • Click Clear All to reset all filters at once

📊 Analytics Tab

  • See yearly vacancy trends with a bar chart
  • Explore the top 10 hottest areas for hiring
  • Discover the most in-demand teaching subjects
  • View a monthly trend line across all years
  • 6 KPI cards summarize key statistics

🔬 Data Quality Tab

  • Visual donut chart: unique vs duplicate ratio
  • Missing data breakdown per field
  • Browse all 7,088 duplicate records with pagination
  • Understand data completeness at a glance

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Ctrl+K — Open search
  • Escape — Close any open modal
  • ? — Open this guide
  • T — Toggle light/dark theme
  • L — Toggle language (EN/AR)

🌐 Language Toggle

  • Click the 🌐 AR button in the top-right navbar
  • Switches the full UI between English and Arabic
  • Arabic mode uses right-to-left (RTL) layout
  • Job cards display original Arabic ad text when available

🌙 Dark / Light Mode

  • Click the moon/sun icon in the top-right corner
  • Your preference is saved automatically for next visit
  • Press T for instant keyboard toggle

📄 Job Card Details

  • Click any card to open the full detail modal
  • See school name, area, subjects, year, contact, and raw ad
  • Copy contact info directly from the modal
  • Duplicate badge shows if a record is a duplicate
