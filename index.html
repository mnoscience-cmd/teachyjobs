TeachyJobs — Complete Guide

🔍 Search & Filter

  • Ctrl+K — Focus search bar from anywhere
  • Search keywords highlight in yellow on matching rows
  • Quick Chips — one-click preset filters (English, Maths, New Cairo…)
  • Contact Type filter — Email, Phone, Has Contact, No Contact
  • Date Range and Year are mutually exclusive — picking one locks the other
  • Clear All resets every filter at once

📋 Vacancy Rows

  • Click ⭐ to save/unsave any vacancy
  • Click 📋 to copy contact info to clipboard instantly
  • Click the school name to see all their postings
  • Click anywhere else on the row to open full details
  • Pink left border = duplicate record
  • Zebra striping helps track rows at a glance

📊 Analytics Tab

  • Yearly bar chart, top areas, top subjects
  • Monthly trend line across all years
  • Subject Demand by Year — grouped chart
  • 6 KPI summary cards

⭐ Saved Tab

  • All starred vacancies appear here
  • Saved jobs persist across sessions (localStorage)
  • Clear all saved with one button
  • Tab shows live count of saved jobs

📁 Export

  • Excel — exports current filtered results as .csv (UTF-8, opens in Excel)
  • TXT — exports as formatted plain text report
  • Exports only the active tab's visible data

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Ctrl+K — Search
  • Esc — Close modal
  • ? — This guide
  • T — Toggle theme
  • L — Toggle language
  • B — Go to Saved tab

🌐 Language & Theme

  • AR/EN toggle — full RTL Arabic mode
  • 🌙 Dark mode saved to localStorage
  • Press T to instantly toggle

🏫 School Profile

  • Click any school name (underlined) to see all their postings
  • School modal shows count, years active, all vacancies
  • Opens as a separate modal over the current view
